Focus

  • Aggregate mismanaged sub-institutional self storage into regional portfolios
  • Operational improvements via best-in-class regional property management relationships
  • Exit/recapitalize into active institutional self storage market

Investment Strategy

1) Aggregate Portfolio

Accumulate regional portfolios of self storage assets with a disciplined focus on going-in basis, untrended yield-on-cost, and reliable in-place cash flow.

2) Operate Efficiently

Implement hybrid remote management and optimize revenue through rent roll improvements and technology.

3) Portfolio Exit

Strategically exit or recapitalize via liquid public REIT / private markets with cap rate arbitrage from institutional scale.
